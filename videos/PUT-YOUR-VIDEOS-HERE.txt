THERE ARE TWO FOLDERS. THIS MATTERS.
فيه فولدرين. دي نقطة مهمة.

  videos\           <- YOUR MASTERS. Big files. NEVER go online.
                       الماسترز بتاعتك. ملفات كبيرة. ماتترفعش على النت.

  videos\web\       <- WHAT THE WEBSITE USES. Small. This is what you upload.
                       اللي الموقع بيستخدمه. صغير. ده اللي بترفعه.

Think of it like masters vs. deliverables. Same idea.

Right now your masters are 382 MB. The web copies are 22 MB.
reel1.mp4 alone was 293 MB (4K, 60fps) - that file would never have
finished loading on Egyptian mobile data. It is now 4.8 MB.


ADDING A NEW VIDEO - the easy way
---------------------------------
1. Drop your export anywhere.
2. Drag it ONTO  COMPRESS-FOR-WEB.bat  (in this folder).
   A black window opens, does the work, and says DONE.
3. The web version appears in  videos\web4. Open admin.html -> Projects -> Add, and pick the new file.

That is it. You never have to think about bitrates.


ADDING A NEW VIDEO - if you'd rather export it yourself
------------------------------------------------------
  Format .......... H.264 MP4
  Resolution ...... 1080x1920 vertical  /  1920x1080 horizontal
  Bitrate ......... 2.5-3 Mbps  (VBR, 2-pass)
  Frame rate ...... 30 fps
  Audio ........... AAC 128 kbps  (KEEP IT - your sound design is the work)
  Target size ..... under 8 MB

Then put the file in  videos\web\  yourself.


BEFORE / AFTER  -  needs TWO files per comparison
-------------------------------------------------
That section is switched OFF right now, because you gave me finished
edits and it needs PAIRS. To switch it on, export the SAME shot twice:

  videos\weba1-before.mp4    <- straight from camera, no grade
  videos\weba1-after.mp4     <- your finished version

Same shot, same length, same first frame. 5-10 seconds. No audio needed
for these two - they play muted side by side.

Then open  assets\js\data.js , find "beforeAfter", and delete the //
in front of the block. The section reappears by itself.


NAMING
------
Lowercase, no spaces, no Arabic in file names.

  GOOD:  crover-real-estate.mp4
  BAD:   Crover (final) 2.mp4


BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
------------------
Upload  videos\web\  only.
Do NOT upload your masters - that is 382 MB of nothing the site uses.


IF A FILE IS MISSING
--------------------
The site shows a message naming the exact file it couldn't find.
It does not break. Fix the name in admin.html and reload.
